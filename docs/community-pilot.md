# Community pilot — draft invitation and feedback guide

Start with three to five teams that already have a customer health or engagement
score and can produce historical feature snapshots plus later, mature outcomes.
The first question is whether they can run and interpret one useful comparison.

## Suggested invitation

> Already have a customer health score? We're looking for a few teams to try a
> scoring-refresh review: compare your current weights with a candidate, test it
> against later outcomes, and see which accounts or segments get worse.
>
> gtm-autoresearch runs locally. Start with our synthetic example, then bring your
> own data if you have suitable historical snapshots. Share what worked, what broke,
> and whether the report helped you decide. You don't need to share customer data.

## Ask each tester

1. Could you complete the synthetic walkthrough without help?
2. Which part of preparing your own data was hardest?
3. Was the decision report clear enough to act on? What was missing?
4. Did it change your decision about a proposed scoring update?
5. What would make you run it again next month?

Use sanitized reproduction steps and synthetic fixtures for public bug reports.
Record unresolved questions alongside successful runs. Do not describe observational
metric improvements as proven incremental revenue or statistical significance.
